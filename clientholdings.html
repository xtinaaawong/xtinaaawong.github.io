Client Holdings: Surfacing data to track tax and risk exposure

BNY Wealth Services is the bank's wealth management division. Our wealth management platform is built around one goal: eliminating the swivel chair effect by putting all necessary data at the user's fingertips. This supports advisors' day-to-day work with efficient processes, freeing up more time for clients and less for admin work.

Process & Thinking

After project intake with Product, I reviewed existing research to understand what our clients' goals are when using this page. We then focused on figuring out the best way to display the data, implementing data visualization categorized by Security Type to surface aggregate data that could flag tax and risk exposure concerns.

From there we moved into smaller but equally important questions: How do we spec the interactions so the important sections stay sticky and visible while maximizing screen real estate? What's the maximum number of security types possible? Should the "Other" card display a gain/loss value for all remaining security types, or would that confuse the user?

We handled discussions through meetings as well as asynchronously, keeping the project moving even with a 12-hour time difference between our working hours.

Hierarchy

One new feature is the ability to track external (aka Held Away) accounts within the platform, giving advisors a more holistic view of their clients.

Filters

To let advisors pull the specific information they need, we kept the filters at the top. These filters affect all the content and data displays below. Users can also group their data by Client Account or by Asset, giving them the flexibility to focus on either how assets are affecting an account, or how many accounts a given asset is affecting.

Various Data Sources

External accounts report data differently than internal accounts. Internal accounts receive real-time data, while external accounts send over a mix of update frequencies. To ensure we're using the most trustworthy data, external accounts display Prior Day data while internal accounts display real-time data. It was also crucial we didn't mix the two sources, since analyzing them together risked improper calculations.
