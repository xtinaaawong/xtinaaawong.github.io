Restrictions: new features with a streamlined multi-user workflow

Clients often have clear instructions on what to invest in and what to avoid. BNY Wealth Services offers this kind of custom control, but the old process made it painful to get. It couldn't handle all requests at once, so advisors ended up repeating the same steps again and again just to get one client's wishes on record. We rebuilt the process into one streamlined flow that connects multiple touchpoints, cutting out the repeat work and giving advisors a faster path to "done".

Workflow B2B
Role Product Designer
Timeline 2024 / 2 months
Company BNY (Bank of New York)

Key Features

  • Streamlined workflow designed for easy field tabbing, built for the Operations team.
  • Liquidate existing assets that are marked as Do Not Buy
  • Apply restrictions to multiple accounts at once
