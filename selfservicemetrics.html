Providing clients with their ESPP metrics on a self-service platform

Carver Edison administers Employee Stock Purchase Plans for public companies. Finance teams at those companies needed access to enrollment and participation data for SEC reporting. That data was only accessible by the internal team at the time, which meant Carver Edison needed to produce these reports periodically... and there was no automation at the time. This feature allowed the client's data to be immediately accessible to them at any time, removing us as the bottleneck to their financial reporting teams.

Data Dashboard Fintech B2B
Role Lead Product Designer
Timeline 2023 / 3 months
Company Carver Edison

Key Features

  • Access to ESPP data anytime, no more waiting 8+ hours for reports
  • Saved 8+ hours of report creation from our internal teams
  • Data immediately available to client for financial reports
